# Project Overview

AI Brand Monitor helps a marketing or support team understand online brand mentions without reading every post manually.

The local application accepts fictional mentions through seed data, manual entry and JSON import. It identifies relevance, classifies sentiment/topic, separates urgency from negativity and highlights items that may require human action. It does not collect from real social platforms.

The owner fixed the final MVP scope to fictional sample data only. Real connectors are outside acceptance scope. The original ATZ document has not been located in this repository; the internal requirements interpretation should be confirmed before claiming complete assignment compliance.

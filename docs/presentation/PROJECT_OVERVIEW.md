# Project Overview

AI Brand Monitor helps a marketing or support team understand online brand mentions without reading every post manually.

The application collects representative mentions, identifies whether they are actually relevant to the monitored brand, classifies sentiment and topic, separates urgency from negativity, and highlights items that may require action.

The MVP focuses on a strong demonstration rather than pretending to be a full social-listening platform. Sample data is sufficient for the assignment, while real integrations remain optional extensions.
